About Us

We are a non-profit organization dedicated to cow conservation and welfare. We promote ethical treatment, sustainable dairy farming, and awareness about the ecological role of cows.

Our Conservation Goals

  • Promote organic and ethical cow farming
  • Provide shelters for abandoned cows
  • Educate the public on the benefits of cow conservation
  • Support rural communities with sustainable practices
